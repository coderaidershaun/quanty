                                                  Forward Pricing


 What should be the fair price for a forward contract? We can answer this ques­
 tion by considering the costs and benefits of buying now compared with buy­
 ing on some future date. In a forward contract, the costs and benefits are not
 eliminated; they are simply deferred. They should therefore be reflected in the
     forward price.
