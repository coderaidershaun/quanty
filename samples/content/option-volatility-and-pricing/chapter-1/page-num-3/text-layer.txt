       The Delta

       The delta (A) is a measure of an option's risk with respect to the direction of
       movement in the underlying contract. A positive delta indicates a desire for
       upward movement; a negative delta indicates a desire for downward move­
       ment. The delta has several different interpretations, any of which may be use­
       ful to a trader depending on the types of strategies being executed.
