Figure 13-4 is a line graph of theoretical profit or loss against underlying price for three spreads. The vertical axis, "Theoretical profit or loss", runs from -50 to +10 in steps of 5. The horizontal axis, "Underlying price", runs from 38 to 58 in steps of 2.

"Spread 1 (short straddle)" is a solid line forming a sharp inverted V peaking near 48.40 at about +5 and falling steeply to about -50 at roughly 44 on the left and 54 on the right. "Spread 2 (ratio spread)" is a dashed line that is flat near 0 to +3 on the left, rises to the peak near 48.40, then drops steeply on the right. "Spread 3 (long butterfly)" is a dotted line forming a smooth bell shape peaking near 48.40 and flattening to about -40 at the far left and right edges.

A circle at the peak, with an arrow, is annotated "At an underlying price of 48.40, all three spreads show approximately the same theoretical profit."

The figure shows that the three spreads give about the same profit at 48.40 but differ in risk for large moves.
