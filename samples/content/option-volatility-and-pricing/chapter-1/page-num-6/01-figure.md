Figure 13-16 Dividend sensitivity. A line chart of theoretical profit or loss against expected quarterly dividend for four spreads. The vertical axis, "Theoretical profit or loss", runs from 0 to +12 in steps of 2. The horizontal axis, "Expected quarterly dividend", runs from 0 to 1.00 in steps of .10. The legend lists "Spread 7 (call calendar spread)" and "Spread 8 (put calendar spread)" as solid lines, "Spread 9 (call diagonal spread)" as a dashed line, and "Spread 10 (put diagonal spread)" as a dotted-dash line. The call calendar spread rises from about +2 at 0 dividend to about +9 at 1.00. The put calendar spread falls from about +11.5 at 0 to 0 at about 0.95. The call diagonal spread (dashed) falls from about +8 at 0 to about +2 at 1.00. The put diagonal spread rises from about +1 at 0 to about +10 at 1.00. All lines cross near a dividend of 0.50 at about +6. The figure shows that call spreads gain and put spreads lose, or the reverse, as the expected dividend increases.
