Figure 24-12 FTSE 100 volatility surface. March 16. 2012. A three-dimensional surface plot of implied volatility against exercise price and months to expiration.

The vertical axis, "Implied volatility", runs from 10% to 45% in steps of 5%. The exercise price axis runs from 4000 to 8000 in steps of 200. The "Months to expiration" axis shows 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17.

The shaded surface is highest at low exercise prices, where implied volatility is far above 30% and falls steeply as the exercise price rises, bottoming out near 10-15% at mid-range strikes. A tall dark ridge rises to the top of the chart, above 45%, at the high-exercise-price end for the near expirations, and the surface is lower and flatter for longer expirations.

The figure shows the volatility skew across exercise prices combined with the term structure across expiration dates, forming a volatility surface.
