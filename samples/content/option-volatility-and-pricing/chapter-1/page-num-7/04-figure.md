Figure 24-12 FTSE 100 volatility surface. March 16. 2012. A three-dimensional surface chart of implied volatility across exercise prices and months to expiration. The vertical axis, "Implied volatility", runs from 10% to 45% in steps of 5%. The "Exercise price" axis runs from 4000 to 8000 in steps of 200. The "Months to expiration" axis shows 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17. The surface is shaded in bands. Implied volatility is highest, about 45% or more, at low exercise prices and short expirations, and falls steeply as exercise price rises toward about 6000 to 6400, where it reaches its lowest, about 10% to 15%. At the highest exercise prices there is a sharp wall rising to about 45% near the 3 to 5 month expirations, and the surface is flatter at longer expirations. The figure demonstrates a volatility skew across exercise prices combined with a term structure across expiration dates.
