An *option* is a contract that gives its holder a right but not a duty. A *call option* gives the right to buy an asset, such as a share of stock, at a fixed price. A *put option* gives the right to sell it at a fixed price. The fixed price is the *strike price*, and the right lasts only until the *expiration*, the date on which the contract ends. Whoever sells the option takes on the matching duty, to trade at that price if the holder asks, and is paid a price, called the premium, for doing so.
