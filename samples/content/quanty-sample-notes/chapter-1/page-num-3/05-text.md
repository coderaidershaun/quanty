*Volatility* is the one input of the model that is not quoted anywhere, so it has to be estimated. One way is to measure how much the stock moved in the past. The other is to turn the problem around: take the market price of the option, ask which volatility makes the Black–Scholes model reproduce it, and read the answer as the opinion of the market. That number is the *implied volatility*, and traders quote option prices in terms of it as often as in dollars.
