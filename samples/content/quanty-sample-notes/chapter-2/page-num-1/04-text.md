The model rests on a short list of assumptions, and each one is a simplification. The stock pays no dividends during the life of the option. Its volatility and the risk-free interest rate are constants. Trading is continuous and costs nothing, and any amount of stock, including a fraction of a share, can be bought or sold short. There is no way to make a risk-free profit. The option is *European*, so it can be exercised only at expiration. Real markets break most of these, and extensions of the model relax them one at a time.
