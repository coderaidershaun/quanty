Let \( V(S, t) \) be the value of the option, which depends on the stock price and on time. Build a portfolio that holds one option and is short \( \Delta \) shares of the stock, so that its value is \( \Pi = V - \Delta S \), where \( \Delta = \partial V / \partial S \). The choice of \( \Delta \) is not arbitrary. It is the amount of stock that makes the random parts of the option and of the stock cancel, as the next step shows.
