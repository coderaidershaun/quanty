Formulas (2.4) and (2.5) are linked. Subtract the put price from the call price and the normal terms combine, leaving a result with no \( \sigma \) in it: a call bought together with a put sold, at the same strike and the same expiration, is worth the stock price minus the strike discounted back to today. This is *put–call parity*. It holds whatever model is used for the stock, so it gives a check on any pair of quoted prices. The two formulas also share the same numbers \( d_1 \) and \( d_2 \), so one calculation of them prices both the call and the put.
